## The first try: an empty drive

At 22:32 the drive held no cartridge: `sg_turs` answered Not Ready, "Medium not present", and
`mt status` showed `DR_OPEN`. The suite's real-drive precondition refused at its first command
(`LIFECYCLE PRECONDITION FAILED: sg_read_attr failed on /dev/sg13`), before the workspace, the erase
or any tapectl command. Nothing was erased; the catalog was not touched; first-run.sh started the
drive-poll timer again and said to run `--upgrade` again, which resumed at upgrade step 9.

Two script defects made it look worse than it was:
- **first-run.sh step 12's preview hides a failed read.** It runs `sg_read_attr | grep … || true`
  and then asks for the medium serial "as printed above". At 22:32 nothing was printed (no
  cartridge); at 23:32 the preview failed again, with "Unit attention" (the first command after
  the cartridge loaded), and again printed nothing. Each time the operator typed the serial from
  memory. The second time the suite's own read, a moment later, verified it.
- **The suite's precondition drops sg_read_attr's own message.** `die "sg_read_attr failed on …"`
  discards the captured output, so "Medium not present" reached the operator only through a
  separate `sg_turs`.

Both are for after (i): the operator knows them now, and a scripts change is proven by the whole
chain before it lands.
